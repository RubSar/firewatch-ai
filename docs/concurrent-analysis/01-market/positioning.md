## Observed positioning

Technosylva markets wildfire and extreme-weather intelligence primarily to utilities and fire agencies, with insurance and credit agencies also represented in its navigation. Its public offering includes flooding and utility resilience as well as wildfire. The site directs visitors toward sales conversations and role-based product discovery. [S01: Homepage](https://technosylva.com/)

## Buyer needs — our interpretation

| Audience | Decision to support | Evidence of value to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire agency analyst | Which incident needs closer attention? | Time to assemble an incident brief; traceability of evidence |
| Utility operations team | Which assets need preparation or mitigation? | Exposure coverage; actionable prioritization |
| Mitigation planner | Where should investment go? | Explainable comparisons of interventions |

These are candidate jobs to validate with users, not interview findings.

## Customer evidence

The vendor's CAL FIRE case study reports nearly 13,000 automated simulations in 2025 and describes integration into field and intelligence workflows. This is evidence of the vendor's claimed operational use, not an independently audited outcome or a benchmark for our product. [S08: CAL FIRE case study](https://technosylva.com/customers/cal-fire/)
