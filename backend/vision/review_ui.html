What counts as a label?

Visible flame: flame directly visible in RGB. Keep smoke/canopy occlusion, ambiguous glow and unresolved edges unknown. Do not infer hidden fire.

Smoke: visible smoke plume. Keep indistinguishable fog/cloud/dust and uncertain translucent edges unknown. Layers can overlap.

Every pixel starts unknown. Negative means you have inspected the pixel and found no visible evidence of the selected target. Add a note explaining any unknown pixels before marking the layer reviewed.

Annotation controls

Changing a mask or notes clears its reviewed status. Undo applies to the current layer (last 20 actions).
